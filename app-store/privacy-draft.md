# MapleSap Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date:
To be set before App Store submission.

MapleSap is operated by Smokey River Studio. MapleSap helps users access sugarbush forecasting, account features, and field feedback tools.

## Information We Collect

Account information:
If you create an account or sign in, MapleSap may collect your email address and authentication information through Supabase.

Feedback information:
If you send feedback, MapleSap may collect your email address, region, and message.

Forecast and app usage information:
MapleSap may process weather query details such as coordinates, station names, region, forecast dates, and related request data to provide forecasts and improve the product.

Device and diagnostic information:
MapleSap may receive basic technical information needed to operate the website, app, and hosting infrastructure, such as IP address, browser or app environment, timestamps, and error details.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:
- Provide MapleSap forecasts and app functionality
- Manage accounts and authentication
- Respond to support and feedback
- Improve reliability, forecasting workflows, and product quality
- Protect the service from abuse or misuse

## Tracking

MapleSap should not track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes. If third-party analytics or advertising tools are enabled later, this policy and the App Privacy questionnaire must be updated before release.

## Data Sharing

MapleSap may use service providers for hosting, authentication, database storage, weather data, and support operations. MapleSap does not sell personal information.

## Data Retention

MapleSap keeps information only as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain records, respond to requests, and meet legal or operational requirements.

## User Choices

Users may contact support to request help with account access, correction, or deletion. Some data may need to be retained where required for security, legal, or operational reasons.

## Children

MapleSap is not intended for children under 13.
