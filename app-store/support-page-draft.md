## MapleSap Support

Need help with MapleSap? Use this page for account, forecast, billing, and product questions.

## Common Topics

Account access:
If sign-in or password reset is not working, include the email address used for your MapleSap account.

Forecast questions:
Include your region, nearest town, and the forecast dates you are checking.

Bug reports:
Include your device, macOS version, browser or app version, what you expected, and what happened.

Billing or premium questions:
Include the email address used for your account. Do not include payment card numbers.

Privacy or account deletion:
Request privacy help or account deletion through support.
