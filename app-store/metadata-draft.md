Description:
MapleSap helps sugar makers plan around the weather patterns that matter most during maple season.

Use MapleSap to watch freeze-thaw windows, compare recent weather, track forecast conditions, and prepare for the next sap run. The app is built for small sugarbush operators, homesteaders, and maple producers who need a simple way to understand when conditions are turning favorable.

MapleSap combines a focused macOS app experience with the live MapleSap forecasting workspace at maplesap.app. It is designed for quick daily checks before tapping, collecting, boiling, or planning field work.

Key features:
- Sap-run focused weather checks
- Freeze-thaw forecasting context
- Sugarbush planning workspace
- Account-based dashboard foundation
- Field feedback collection
- Desktop access to the live MapleSap app
