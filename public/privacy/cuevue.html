CueVue

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 10, 2026

1 — Overview

About CueVue

CueVue is a macOS desktop application developed by Smokey River Studio. It helps presenters, educators, and product teams manage live demonstrations, cue their content, and run polished on-screen experiences.

Minimal data collection: CueVue is a local-first application. Your presentations, scripts, and cue data are stored on your own device. We do not upload your content to our servers.

This policy describes the limited system permissions CueVue requires and the minimal information that may be transmitted during use.

2 — Information We Collect

In its current version, CueVue collects:

  • Crash reports and error logs (if you opt in to diagnostics)
  • App version, macOS version, and hardware architecture (for crash diagnosis)
  • Anonymous feature usage events (e.g., "cue triggered") — no content is included

Crash reports do not include the content of your presentations, recordings, or personal files.

3 — Information We Do Not Collect

CueVue does not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your name, email address, or account credentials (no account required)
  • The content of your screen recordings, presentations, or scripts
  • Camera video or microphone audio streams
  • Your location or IP address beyond standard server log metadata
  • Any personally identifiable information linked to usage behaviour

4 — System Permissions

🖥 Screen Recording

CueVue requires macOS Screen Recording permission to capture and manage on-screen content for cued presentations and demonstrations.

What this permission does: Allows CueVue to read screen pixel data to power its cue detection and presentation management features.

What we do not do: Screen capture data is processed locally on your Mac. No screen content is transmitted to our servers or any third party.

You can revoke this permission at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording. Revoking will disable CueVue's core functionality.

📷 Camera

CueVue may request camera access for features that include webcam preview or presenter-mode overlays.

What this permission does: Provides a live camera feed within the CueVue interface for use during presentations.

What we do not do: Camera footage is not recorded, stored, or transmitted. The feed is rendered locally for the duration of your session only.

You can revoke this permission at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

🎙 Microphone

CueVue may request microphone access for features such as voice-triggered cues or audio monitoring during live presentations.

What this permission does: Provides access to the microphone input stream for real-time use within CueVue's cue engine.

What we do not do: Audio is processed locally and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted to any external server.

You can revoke this permission at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

5 — Local Storage

CueVue stores the following data on your local device:

  • Application preferences and settings
  • Saved cue configurations and presentation projects
  • Recent file history
  • Window layout and UI state

All locally stored data remains on your Mac and is subject to your device's own security and backup settings. CueVue does not sync this data to any cloud service.

You can clear CueVue's local data by uninstalling the application and removing its associated Application Support folder.

6 — Analytics & Diagnostics

CueVue may transmit anonymized diagnostic information if you opt in. This includes:

  • App version and macOS version
  • Crash stack traces (no personal data or file content)
  • Anonymous feature usage counts (no content, no identity)

Diagnostic reporting is opt-in and can be disabled in CueVue's Preferences. If disabled, no data is transmitted to our servers.

7 — Third-Party Services

CueVue may use the following third-party services:

  • Paddle or Apple Mac App Store — payment processing for licence purchases (if applicable)
  • Sentry or similar — opt-in crash reporting (no personal data included)

CueVue does not integrate advertising SDKs, social media SDKs, or user tracking frameworks of any kind.

8 — Data Retention

Because CueVue is a local-first application, most data never leaves your device. For the limited diagnostic data we receive:

  • Crash reports: Retained for 90 days, then automatically deleted
  • Anonymous usage events: Aggregated and retained for up to 12 months
  • Purchase records: Retained as required for tax and accounting obligations (7 years, Canadian law)

9 — Children's Privacy

CueVue is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10 — Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as CueVue adds new features. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice or update release notes. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
