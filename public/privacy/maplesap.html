MapleSAP

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 10, 2026

1 — Overview

Who We Are

MapleSAP is a mobile application developed by Smokey River Studio, a Canadian software studio. MapleSAP provides weather-informed forecasting tools to help maple syrup producers plan their tapping and harvest activities.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data. We comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation.

Plain English: We collect the minimum information necessary to deliver the service. We do not sell your data. We do not use your information for advertising.

2 — Account Information

What We Collect

To create and manage your MapleSAP account, we collect:

  • Email address (used for sign-in and service communications)
  • Password (stored as a one-way cryptographic hash — never in plain text)
  • Account creation date and last login timestamp
  • Subscription tier (Free or Pro)

Why We Collect It

Account information is required to authenticate you, associate your sugar bush locations with your profile, and deliver features tied to your subscription plan.

What We Do Not Collect

  • Your legal name or mailing address (not required)
  • Phone number (not required)
  • Government-issued identification

3 — Location Data

Device GPS

MapleSAP may request access to your device location to identify your nearest weather station and deliver region-specific sap forecasts. Location access is optional — you may enter a location manually instead.

We request location access only while the app is in use (when-in-use permission). We do not request background location access.

Sugar Bush Locations

When you add a sugar bush to your account, you may provide GPS coordinates or a named location. This information is stored on our servers associated with your account and is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver localized forecasts for that bush
  • Display your bush locations within the app
  • Calculate seasonal statistics for your property

Sugar bush location data is never shared, sold, or used to infer unrelated personal information.

4 — Forecast & Weather Data

Forecast data (temperature, solar radiation, freeze/thaw cycle predictions) is sourced from third-party meteorological services. When you request a forecast, we transmit your region or coordinates to our weather data provider. We do not transmit your account identity alongside location queries.

Forecast results are cached on our servers to reduce redundant API calls and improve performance. Cached forecast data is not linked to identifiable user records beyond your region of interest.

5 — Push Notifications

MapleSAP may send push notifications to alert you to sap-run conditions, freeze/thaw events, and season-ending warm streaks. Push notification delivery requires a device push token, which is stored on our servers associated with your account.

You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's notification settings or within the MapleSAP app. Disabling notifications does not affect your ability to use the app.

6 — Subscription Services

MapleSAP offers a paid Pro subscription. Subscription management is handled through the platform's native billing system:

  • iOS: Apple App Store (StoreKit in-app purchase)
  • Android: Google Play Billing

We do not directly handle or store your payment card details. All billing transactions occur through Apple or Google's payment infrastructure, subject to their respective privacy policies.

We receive confirmation of your subscription status (active, expired, cancelled) and your subscription tier. We do not receive your card number, bank details, or billing address.

7 — Analytics

We may collect aggregated, non-identifying analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve reliability. This may include:

  • Feature usage frequency (which screens are visited, which features are used)
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • App version and device OS version
  • General region (country/province — not precise GPS)

Analytics data is aggregated and not linked to your identity. We do not use analytics to build advertising profiles or share data with ad networks.

8 — Data Retention

We retain your account and associated data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Specifically:

  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Sugar bush locations: Deleted when you remove them or delete your account
  • Forecast cache: Automatically purged after 48 hours
  • Push tokens: Removed when you log out or uninstall the app
  • Crash logs: Automatically deleted after 90 days

When you delete your account, we permanently delete your email address, sugar bush locations, and all associated data within 30 days. Anonymized aggregate data (e.g., "users in Ontario") is not deleted, as it contains no personal identifiers.

9 — Your Rights (PIPEDA)

As a Canadian resident, you have rights under PIPEDA and applicable provincial legislation. You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw consent to optional data uses (e.g., analytics)
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

10 — Third-Party Services

MapleSAP integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Weather data provider — receives region/coordinates to return forecast data
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — handles in-app subscriptions and billing
  • Apple Push Notification Service / Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications
  • Cloudflare — CDN and hosting infrastructure (processes request metadata)

We select service providers that meet our privacy and security standards. We do not share personal information with third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics platforms that build individual user profiles.

11 — Children's Privacy

MapleSAP is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

12 — Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you via the app or by email. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use of MapleSAP after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
