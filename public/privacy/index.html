MapleSAP

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 10, 2026

Current Version Notice — v1.0.1

The current release of mapleSAP (v1.0.1) is a sample-data preview. In this version:

  • No user accounts are created or authenticated.
  • No personal information is collected.
  • No location data is requested or transmitted.
  • All forecasts and bush data shown are illustrative sample data only.
  • No data of any kind is sent to Smokey River Studio servers.

The sections below describe data practices that will apply when full account, location, and forecasting features are enabled in a future release. They do not describe behaviour of the current version.

1 — Overview

Who We Are

MapleSAP is a mobile application developed by Smokey River Studio, a Canadian software studio. MapleSAP provides weather-informed forecasting tools to help maple syrup producers plan their tapping and harvest activities.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. We comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation.

Plain English: We collect the minimum information necessary to deliver the service. We do not sell your data. We do not use your information for advertising.

2 — Account Information

What We Collect

  • Email address — used for sign-in and service communications
  • Password — stored as a one-way cryptographic hash, never in plain text
  • Account creation date and last login timestamp
  • Subscription tier (Free or Pro)

What We Do Not Collect

  • Your legal name or mailing address
  • Phone number
  • Government-issued identification

3 — Location Data

Device GPS

MapleSAP may request access to your device location to identify your nearest weather station and deliver region-specific sap forecasts. Location access is optional — you may enter a location manually instead.

We request when-in-use location permission only. We do not request background location access.

Sugar Bush Locations

When you add a sugar bush, we store its GPS coordinates or named location on our servers, associated with your account. This is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver localized forecasts for that bush
  • Display your bush locations within the app
  • Calculate seasonal statistics for your property

Sugar bush location data is never shared, sold, or used to infer unrelated personal information.

4 — Forecast & Weather Data

Forecast data is sourced from third-party meteorological services. When you request a forecast, we transmit your region or coordinates to our weather data provider. We do not transmit your account identity alongside location queries.

Forecast results are cached on our servers to reduce redundant API calls. Cached data is not linked to identifiable user records beyond your region of interest.

5 — Push Notifications

MapleSAP may send push notifications for sap-run conditions, freeze/thaw events, and season-ending warm streaks. Push delivery requires a device push token stored on our servers, associated with your account.

You can disable push notifications at any time through your device settings or within the app. Disabling notifications does not affect your access to the app.

6 — Subscription & Payment

MapleSAP offers a paid Pro subscription. Billing is handled through the platform's native system:

  • iOS: Apple App Store in-app purchase (StoreKit)
  • Android: Google Play Billing

We do not directly handle or store your payment card details. All billing occurs through Apple or Google's payment infrastructure. We receive confirmation of your subscription status and tier only — not your card number, bank details, or billing address.

7 — Analytics

We may collect aggregated, non-identifying analytics to understand how the app is used:

  • Feature usage frequency
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • App version and device OS version
  • General region (country/province — not precise GPS)

Analytics data is not linked to your identity and is not shared with advertising networks.

8 — Third-Party Services

  • Weather data provider — receives region/coordinates to return forecast data
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — handles subscriptions and billing
  • Apple Push Notification Service / Firebase Cloud Messaging — push delivery
  • Cloudflare — CDN and hosting infrastructure

We do not share personal information with advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics platforms that build individual user profiles.

9 — Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Sugar bush locations: Deleted when you remove them or delete your account
  • Forecast cache: Automatically purged after 48 hours
  • Push tokens: Removed when you log out or uninstall the app
  • Crash logs: Automatically deleted after 90 days

When you delete your account, we permanently delete your email address, sugar bush locations, and all associated data within 30 days.

10 — Your Rights (PIPEDA)

As a Canadian resident, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw consent to optional data uses
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

11 — Children's Privacy

MapleSAP is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have done so inadvertently, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12 — Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the app or email. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
